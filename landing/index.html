Product tour

  1. Dispatch desk

    Enter five call conditions. Get a calibrated risk score, a rank against the shift, and the next question worth asking.

  2. Pulse

    Exposure-adjusted hotspots show where serious collisions cluster relative to traffic, not just where roads are busy.

  3. Control room

    A live queue sorted by risk, with AML and eCall messages parsed straight into the form.

  4. Capacity planner

    A digital twin replays real demand to compare risk-ranked dispatch with first-come-first-served.

  5. Shift review

    Precision at K for every shift, plus a handover brief written for the incoming supervisor.
